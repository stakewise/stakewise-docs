# Simple Staking

The simplest way to stake with StakeWise is through the [StakeWise App ↗](https://app.stakewise.io) — deposit any amount of ETH and stake it in a single click. Your assets are routed through a [MetaVault](/docs/vaults/vault-types#metavault) that distributes them across the best-performing sub-vaults. Today that chain resolves to the [Genesis Vault ↗](https://app.stakewise.io/vault/mainnet/0xac0f906e433d58fa868f936e8a43230473652885), with more sub-vaults to come.

When staking, you'll automatically receive [osETH](/docs/ostoken/intro), which represents your staked assets plus accumulated rewards. osETH can be used [across DeFi to boost your yield ↗](https://app.stakewise.io/ecosystem).

## How to Stake

Stake your ETH and start earning rewards immediately.

<Image img={require('./img/staking_flow.png')} alt="Staking flow explanation diagram" />

1. Open [app.stakewise.io ↗](https://app.stakewise.io) and connect your wallet.
2. Select the **Stake** tab and enter the amount of ETH you want to stake.
3. Choose what happens to the stake:
    - **Stake only** — your ETH is staked and no osETH is minted.
    - **Receive osETH** — osETH is minted against your stake. This is the default.
    - **Receive and boost osETH** — the newly minted osETH is locked as [Boost](/staker/boost) collateral in the same transaction. Available from 0.05 ETH.
4. Review the details.
5. Click **Stake** and confirm the transaction in your wallet.
6. Once confirmed, your stake immediately starts earning rewards. Unless you chose **Stake only**, osETH appears in your wallet.

:::custom-tips[Track Your Position Anytime]
In the **Balance** tab you can view your <Tooltip content="The total amount of ETH that is earning rewards.">total staked</Tooltip> amount and your <Tooltip content="The percentage you earn on your staked ETH after fees.">APY</Tooltip>. If you have an active Boost position, the APY figure reflects it.
:::

## Boost

Once you have osETH in your wallet, you can optionally [Boost](/staker/boost) your position to amplify your staking yield. Boost uses your osETH as collateral on Aave to borrow ETH, which is then restaked automatically.

## How to Unstake

Unstaking is just as simple — convert your osETH back to ETH directly in the StakeWise App whenever you want.

osETH can only be **minted** or **burned** — staking mints new osETH, and unstaking burns it to release the underlying ETH. osETH bought on a DEX isn't tied to any staking position, so it won't appear in the **Unstake** tab. To convert market-bought osETH back to ETH, swap it on the [Swap page ↗](https://app.stakewise.io/swap) of the StakeWise App or sell it on a DEX.

Before unstaking, make sure the amount of osETH you want to unstake is in your wallet. If you have an active Boost position, switch to the **Unboost** tab and exit it first.

<Image img={require('./img/unstaking_flow.png')} alt="Unstaking flow explanation diagram" />

1. Open [app.stakewise.io ↗](https://app.stakewise.io) and connect your wallet.
2. Select the **Unstake** tab and enter the amount of osETH you want to convert back to ETH.
3. Review the details.
4. Click **Unstake** and confirm the transaction in your wallet.
5. Your request enters the Vault's [exit queue](/docs/vaults/how-vaults-work#withdrawals). The app shows a live estimate of how long it will take, along with the average over the past month — how long it actually takes depends on the network's validator exit queue. Your assets keep earning rewards until the exit completes.
6. Once the request is processed, click **Claim** and confirm the transaction to receive your ETH (original stake plus accumulated rewards).

:::custom-tips[Get a Claim Alert]
You can set up an email or browser notification to be alerted as soon as your unstaked assets are ready to be claimed. Click your wallet address in the top-right corner, then click the alert button <img src="/icons/stakewise/alert_button.png" alt="alert button icon" style={{height: '2.5em', verticalAlign: 'middle', display: 'inline-block'}} /> next to your address. In the **Configure alerts** pop-up, enter your email and click **Save**, or enable browser notifications.
:::
